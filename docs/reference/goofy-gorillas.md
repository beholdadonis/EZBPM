# Goofy Gorillas reference screenshots

53 screenshots of the play place in **Goofy Gorillas**, the Steam game ours
is inspired by: 11 from its Steam store page, 32 uploaded by players on its
Steam community page, and 10 frames from its official trailer. They show
how its map **feels**: one giant connected play structure, nets everywhere,
dark building, colored light, cluttered floor.

**Use them for the feel only. Never copy anything.** Our game is fully
original (design doc section 1). Don't copy their logos, wall signs and
slogans (you'll see their brand name on walls), their mascot, their
characters or their exact layout. Our layout and look are in `GG.md`
Part 3.

## Getting the pictures

They are **not stored in this repo** (the repo is public and they're
another game's art). Download them with:

```
bash docs/reference/get-goofy-gorillas-screenshots.sh
```

They land in `docs/reference/goofy-gorillas/`, which git ignores. The
script needs `curl`; the 10 trailer frames also need `ffmpeg` and are
skipped without it. Run it again any time; it skips pictures it already has.

Sources: the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2627570/Goofy_Gorillas/)
(screenshots and trailer) and the
[community screenshots page](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2627570/screenshots/).
